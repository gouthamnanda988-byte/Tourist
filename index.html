TRAVEL ADVISORY GUIDELINES

Tourist Safety Instructions by Terrain & Hazard

Essential precautionary standard operating procedures for domestic and international travelers visiting India.

🌊

Monsoon & Coastal Surges

Beaches, river valleys, and lowlands
  • Respect Red Flags on Beaches: Never enter sea waters when lifeguards fly red caution flags (common in Goa, Puri, Kovalam, Chennai).
  • Avoid Riverbank Camping: Mountain rivers (Beas, Ganga, Teesta) swell rapidly without warning due to upstream cloudbursts.
  • Steer Clear of Waterlogged Roads: Avoid driving through standing water; hidden manholes and submerged bridge causeways pose severe hazards.
  • Monitor High Tide Timings: Coastal strolls in Mumbai or Rameshwaram during high tides can suddenly isolate tourists on rock formations.
🏔️

Mountain & Landslide Protocol

Himachal, Uttarakhand, Kashmir, Sikkim
  • Acclimatize Gradually: At altitudes above 2,500m (Leh, Gulmarg, Rohtang), take 24–48 hours of rest to prevent Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS).
  • Watch Falling Rock Triggers: Landslides occur abruptly on steep cuts during or right after rainfall. Never halt your vehicle under loose scree slopes.
  • Hire Registered Mountain Guides: Only trek with guides certified by the Indian Mountaineering Foundation (IMF) or State Tourism Boards.
  • Check Highway Pass Permits: Mountain passes (Atal Tunnel, Zoji La, Khardung La) close dynamically based on black ice and weather.
☀️

Heatwave & Desert Survival

Rajasthan, Delhi NCR, Central Plains
  • Electrolyte Hydration: Drink Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS), coconut water, or nimbu pani regularly, even if not feeling thirsty.
  • Avoid Peak Afternoon Sun (12 PM – 4 PM): Schedule fort visits and outdoor monuments in early morning hours (6 AM – 10 AM) or sunset.
  • Dress in Loose, Breathable Cotton: Full sleeves shield against UV burns and hot desert winds (the infamous 'Loo').
  • Identify Heatstroke Warning Signs: Confusion, nausea, cessation of sweat, or high fever require immediate cold packs and emergency 108 medical care.
🛡️

Women & Solo Tourist Safeguards

Pan-India travel security protocols
  • Save Dedicated Women Helpline 1091: Program 1091 and 112 on speed dial with location sharing enabled in messaging apps.
  • Use Verified Transit & Prepaid Counters: At airports and railway stations, opt for government prepaid taxi booths or verified app-based cabs.
  • Female Compartments & Waiting Rooms: Indian Railways and metro systems (Delhi, Chennai, Mumbai) provide designated women coaches and security guards.
  • Verify Hotel Registration: Check that your accommodation is officially approved by the Ministry of Tourism or reputable state tourism corporations.

HACKATHON ARCHITECTURE & VISION

About TouristSafe India

India is one of the world's most geographically diverse tourist landscapes—spanning Himalayan glaciers, desert plains, dense rainforests, and thousands of kilometers of coastline.

TouristSafe was engineered as a rapid emergency management dashboard prototype designed to solve critical tourist information vacuums during flash floods, cyclonic swells, sudden heatwaves, or mountain landslides.

Built strictly with semantic, lightweight, dependency-free vanilla web technologies (HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript ES6+), it provides frictionless performance even across low-bandwidth 2G/3G mobile networks in remote travel corridors.

Semantic HTML5 Modular CSS3 Grid/Flexbox Vanilla JavaScript ES6+ Tri-lingual (EN / தமிழ் / हिन्दी) Zero External Frameworks Local Development Ready

Project Implementation Highlights

  • 🌦️ Real-Time Responsive Cards: Instant thermal, moisture, precipitation, and wind vector status with normalized ranges.
  • 🚨 Tiered Emergency Severity: Categorized Red (High), Orange (Moderate), and Blue (Advisory) warnings with verified action steps.
  • 🌐 Full Tri-lingual Switching: Dynamically toggles all interfaces between English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिन्दी) in-memory.
  • 🔊 Web Audio API Siren: Integrated browser audio synthesizer for simulating emergency alert siren tones offline.
  • 📱 Fully Mobile Responsive: Touch-friendly controls, quick SOS drawer, and legible typography across all screen ratios.

India All-Emergency Dispatch: 112

Police • Ambulance • Fire • Disaster Rescue
When calling 112, be prepared to clearly state:
  1. Your precise current location (City, Road/NH, Landmark, or Hotel name).
  2. Nature of hazard: Medical emergency, Landslide, Flash flood, or Crime.
  3. Number of persons affected and if children or seniors require urgent assistance.
  4. Your preferred language: Operators are trained to dispatch in Hindi, English, and regional languages.
